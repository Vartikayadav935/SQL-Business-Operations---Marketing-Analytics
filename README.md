📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard to analyze revenue, profitability, profit margins, and marketing channel performance. The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation Using SQL
- Imported and structured the sales dataset using SQL.
- Checked for missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent data.
- Verified data types and ensured numerical columns were correctly formatted.
- Performed data validation to maintain accuracy and consistency.
- Prepared clean and structured data for dashboard development.

📈 Dashboard Development Using Power BI
Designed an interactive dashboard featuring:

- Gross Revenue:Overall revenue generated across marketing channels.
- Net Revenue:Revenue after accounting for losses and deductions.
- Total Profit:Overall profitability analysis.
- Profit Margin:Evaluated profitability across different channels.
- Revenue Loss:Identified revenue reduction across marketing channels.
- Channel Performance:Compared Paid Search, Direct, Email, Organic Search, Referral, and Social Media.

🔍 Key Business Insights
- Analyzed revenue contribution across six marketing channels.
- Identified high-performing and low-performing marketing channels.
- Compared gross revenue against net revenue.
- Evaluated profit margins to understand channel profitability.
- Examined revenue losses and overall profit contribution.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- SQL – Data Cleaning, Data Validation & Data Preparation
- Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard Development & Visualization
- DAX – KPI Calculations and Measures
- CSV – Dataset Management

🎯 Project Outcome
Successfully transformed structured sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard, providing clear visibility into sales performance, revenue trends, profitability, and marketing channel effectiveness.

**Skills Demonstrated:** SQL, Data Cleaning, Data Analysis, Power BI, DAX, Data Visualization, KPI Development, and Business Intelligence.
